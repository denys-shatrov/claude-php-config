# NNNN. Short title stating the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or roles

## Context

The forces at play: the requirement, the constraint, the problem that made a
choice necessary. State it so that someone who disagrees with the outcome would
still recognise the situation as accurate.

Include what was true at the time — scale, team size, deadlines, existing
systems. That context is what lets a future reader judge whether the decision
still holds.

## Options considered

### Option A — name

What it is. Why it was attractive. Why it was not chosen.

### Option B — name

The same, honestly. Do not weaken an option to justify the choice; an ADR that
strawmans its alternatives is useless when the question returns.

## Decision

One sentence, active voice: "We use X."

Then a short paragraph on what tipped it.

## Consequences

**Good:** what this buys.

**Bad:** what becomes harder, slower, or more expensive. What you are now
locked into. If this section is empty, the decision has not been examined.

**Follow-up:** work this creates — migrations, documentation, a review date.
